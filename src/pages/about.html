Biography

Central University of Kerala, Kasaragod

Ravi Kumar is a Senior Research Fellow and PhD candidate in the Department of International Relations and Political Science at the Central University of Kerala. His doctoral thesis, Status, Regions, and Rising Powers in the Emerging World Order: A Case Study of India, has been submitted, and the viva is awaited.

His research examines status and recognition in international relations, region formation and rising-power strategy, India’s foreign and nuclear policy, and the Indo-Pacific. The thesis develops Regional Status Theory, which holds that rising powers build and lead regions to secure recognition, and tests it across four Indian cases from 1945 to the present using archival sources, a coded corpus of parliamentary debate and Ministry of External Affairs records. His articles are published in or forthcoming from India Review, World Affairs and other journals, and he has released open datasets of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha debates on Harvard Dataverse.

He holds an MPhil in Political Science from the University of Rajasthan, and an MA in Political Science (University Gold Medal) and an MSc in Mathematics from Maharshi Dayanand University. He is UGC-NET qualified in Political Science (December 2019, with Junior Research Fellowship) and in International and Area Studies.
