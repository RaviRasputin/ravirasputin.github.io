UGC NET Paper 1 preparation programme (2026)

Convened by Ravi Kumar at the Department of International Relations and Political Science, Central University of Kerala. Research scholars from other departments were invited to teach units of the Paper 1 syllabus to research scholars and postgraduate students.

Schedule
Mondays and Wednesdays, 3:30–5:00 PM (two 45-minute sessions), from 24 September to 30 November 2026, with a break for the university’s end-semester examinations (16–28 November).
Venue
In person, Cauvery Block, Room 221; also online through Google Meet.
Units covered
Research aptitude; comprehension and communication; mathematical aptitude and reasoning; data interpretation; information and communication technology; people, development and environment; higher education system. Students were also given study materials and a period for doubt-clearing before the December examination.
