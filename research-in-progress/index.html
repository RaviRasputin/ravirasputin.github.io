Projects

Doctoral thesis: region-building as status strategy

Thesis submitted 28 September 2026 · viva awaited

Status, Regions, and Rising Powers in the Emerging World Order: A Case Study of India. The thesis develops Regional Status Theory: rising powers build and lead regions to secure recognition, rather than acquiring regional leadership as a by-product of rising. The theory is tested across four Indian cases from 1945 to the present, using archival sources, a coded corpus of parliamentary debate and Ministry of External Affairs records.

Supervisor
Dr Sivakumar M V, Central University of Kerala.
Draws on
Parliamentary debate datasets and Ministry of External Affairs records.

Deliberation and nuclear policy: India’s Parliament, 1948–2025

Book-length study in progress

A book-length study of how Indian nuclear policy was argued on the floor of Parliament from 1948 to 2025, drawing on the parliamentary corpus built for the doctoral thesis.

Draws on
Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha debate datasets.
