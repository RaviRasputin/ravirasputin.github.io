Research

Research Profile

His research asks how rising powers seek recognition of higher rank in international politics. His doctoral thesis develops Regional Status Theory: rising powers build and lead regions to secure recognition, rather than acquiring regional leadership as a by-product of rising. He tests the theory across four Indian cases from 1945 to the present, using archival sources, a coded corpus of parliamentary debate and Ministry of External Affairs records.

A second line of work studies how India’s foreign and nuclear policy has been argued in Parliament, using open corpora of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha debates from 1952 to the present. A third examines status competition and maritime strategy in the Indo-Pacific, including India’s SAGAR initiative.
